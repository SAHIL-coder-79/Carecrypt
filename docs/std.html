Chapter 1 of 1

Quick practice — reveal an answer

1. With even parity, what parity bit goes with 1011?

There are three 1s, an odd count. Add parity bit 1 to make four 1s, an even count.

2. A noiseless channel has bandwidth 3 kHz and 4 signal levels. What is its Nyquist maximum?

Nyquist: 2B log₂L = 2 × 3000 × log₂4 = 6000 × 2 = 12,000 bits/s, or 12 kb/s.

3. Which ARQ retransmits only the missing or damaged frame?

Selective Repeat. Go-Back-N resends the error frame and later frames in its window; Stop-and-Wait has only one outstanding frame.

4. What does the receiver do with a Hamming syndrome of 101?

Read it as binary 5: the error is at position 5 (with the stated position numbering). Flip that bit.
